Visual Arts

While Patricia may not draw or paint, she channels her aesthetic passion through the visual arts of photography and modeling. As a hobbyist photographer, her artistic eye is drawn toward capturing the beauty of nature, often exploring perspective, reflections, and symmetry. She also enjoys photographing animals and striking architectural designs.


Her photography can be found on various artist websites including:
Behance, Tumblr, DeviantArt, ViewBug, 500px, Steller, VSCO, and Flickr.
